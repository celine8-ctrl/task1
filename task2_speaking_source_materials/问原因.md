  - 人是善变的（Changeable）
    - People are naturally changeable,people’s opinions on the same thing shift over time because new experiences or conversations with friends can totally flip their mindset.
      - 人是天生多变的，人们对同一事物的看法会随着时间的推移而改变，因为新的经历或与朋友的谈话可以完全改变他们的心态。
    - Life events normally reshape our values—like becoming a parent might turn a carefree person into caring person.
      - 生活事件通常会重塑我们的价值观——比如成为父母可能会把无忧无虑的人变成有爱心的人。
  - 利己的精英主义（Self-centered elitism）
    - Some adopt a ‘survival of the fittest’ mindset—In competitive fields, people might prioritize personal success over teamwork—it’s all about individual glory.
      - 有些人采取“适者生存”的心态——在竞争领域，人们可能会优先考虑个人成功而不是团队合作——这都是关于个人荣耀的。
  - 攀比心作祟 The mind of rivalry at play
    - In modern society, influenced by social media and consumerism, people tend to compare themselves with others.
      - 在现代社会，受社交媒体和消费主义的影响，人们倾向于将自己与他人进行比较。
    - They compare the cars they drive, the clothes they wear, or the vacations they take. This can create unnecessary stress and dissatisfaction.
      - 他们比较他们驾驶的汽车、他们穿的衣服或他们度假的假期。这可能会造成不必要的压力和不满。
    - For instance, if someone sees their friend posting about an expensive overseas trip on social media, they may feel inadequate and pressured to do the same, even if it's beyond their financial means.
      - 例如，如果有人看到他们的朋友在社交媒体上发布关于昂贵的海外旅行的帖子，他们可能会感到不足和压力，即使这超出了他们的经济能力。
    - This constant comparison robs them of their own sense of self - worth and happiness.
      - 这种不断的比较剥夺了他们自己的自我价值感和幸福感。
  - 人生的自我价值实现 (find your passion)
    - Finding your passion is crucial for self actualization. When you discover what truly excites you, whether it's painting, teaching, or helping the poor, you can channel your energy into it.
      - 找到你的激情对于自我实现至关重要。当你发现真正让你兴奋的是什么时，无论是绘画、教学还是帮助穷人，你都可以将精力投入其中。
    - This pursuit allows you to express your unique talents and make a positive impact.
      - 这种追求可以让你表达你独特的才能，并产生积极的影响。
    - For example, an artist who is passionate about painting can create works that touch people's hearts, and in the process, feel a deep sense of fulfillment and self - worth.
      - 例如，一个对绘画充满热情的艺术家可以创作出能打动人心的作品，并在此过程中感受到深深的成就感和自我价值感。
    - It's not just about achieving external success but also about finding internal meaning and purpose in what you do.
      - 这不仅仅关乎取得外在的成功，更在于在你所做的事情中找到内在的意义与目标。
  - 心理健康 Mental Health Issues/Problems
    - 童年创伤（Childhood Trauma）
      - Traumatic childhood experiences, such as neglect or harsh parenting, can shape adult behaviors.
        - 童年的创伤性经历，如忽视或苛刻的育儿，会塑造成年人的行为。
      - For instance, someone overly anxious about criticism might have been constantly scolded as a child. Therapy helps reframe these memories.
        - 例如，一个对批评过于焦虑的人小时候可能会不断被责骂。治疗有助于重新构建这些记忆。
    - 控制欲（Need for Control）
      - Excessive control often stems from fear of uncertainty. Because human like a sense of control .
        - 过度控制往往源于对不确定性的恐惧。因为人类喜欢一种控制感。
      - A micromanaging boss might fear failure, while a parent controlling a child’s choices may project their own unfulfilled dreams.
        - 管理微观的老板可能会害怕失败，而控制孩子选择的父母可能会投射出自己未实现的梦想。

      - 激烈的竞争让人筋疲力尽，每个人都为了脱颖而出而过度工作。
  - 躺平 Lying Flat
    - some practice quiet quitting mentality — they do the bare minimum to protect their peace, refusing overtime without guilt.
      - 一些人实践着“安静离职”的心态——他们只做最低限度的工作以保护自己的内心平静，毫无愧疚地拒绝加班。
  - 从众 Herd Mentality
    - Brainwashing on social media makes people follow the trend without thinking. This is a case of herd mentality.
      - 社交媒体上的洗脑让人们不加思考地随大流。这是从众心理的一个例证。

> 适用范围：现行题库24个主题、122道题。旧题保留区13道题不纳入本次统计和背诵。

## 二、看到题目后的三步反应

1. 看触发词，在两秒内选定题型代码。
2. 按索引调用一个素材模块，不需要复述模块全文。
3. 说三句：**直接回答 → 原因 → 个人细节或例子**。目标为25–45词；一次只用一种复杂结构。

若真实答案是否定的，保留骨架但替换第一句即可，例如：`Not really, mainly because…`。不要为了使用素材而改变立场。

## 三、八类回答骨架

### L｜喜好类

- 触发词：like, enjoy, favourite, want
- 骨架（15词）：Absolutely. I am a huge fan of [topic], mainly because [reason]. For example, [personal detail].
- 完整示例（34词）：Absolutely. I am a huge fan of reading because it helps me switch off after a demanding shift. For example, even twenty minutes with a novel can recharge my batteries and improve my mood.

### F｜频率类

- 触发词：how often, usually, every day, lately
- 骨架（16词）：I do it quite often, usually when [situation], because it helps me [benefit] in daily life.
- 完整示例（29词）：I walk three or four times a week after work. There is a park near my apartment where the fresh air helps me reduce stress and stay reasonably active.

### E｜经历类

- 触发词：have you ever, did you, when did
- 骨架（15词）：Definitely. I remember there was a time when [experience], which made me feel [result] afterwards.
- 完整示例（28词）：Definitely. At college, I led an all-female rowing team which trained on the sea. It was demanding, but achieving goals together gave me a genuine sense of pride.

### I｜重要性类

- 触发词：important, necessary, should
- 骨架（16词）：Without a doubt, [topic] plays a significant role because [reason], especially when [situation] in daily life.
- 完整示例（29词）：Without a doubt, organisation matters in nursing because it saves time and reduces mistakes. It is especially important during a busy shift, when patient safety depends on careful preparation.

### N｜本国现象类

- 触发词：in your country, in China, culture, popular
- 骨架（16词）：Generally speaking, it is fairly common in China these days, mainly because [cultural or practical reason].
- 完整示例（26词）：Keeping pets is fairly common in Chinese cities because animals provide companionship. However, busy workers may hesitate, since responsible pet care requires considerable time and energy.

### C｜选择对比类

- 触发词：prefer, or, difference, change
- 骨架（17词）：It depends on the situation. I choose A when [condition], whereas B suits [different condition] for me.
- 完整示例（27词）：I prefer typing when I need speed and efficiency, whereas handwriting suits short notes that I want to remember. For exam preparation, typing is usually more practical.

### G｜列举类

- 触发词：what kinds, what types, what things
- 骨架（15词）：There are several things, but the main one is [point], since it helps people [benefit].
- 完整示例（27词）：I visit several websites, but nursing and language-learning sites are the main ones. They provide reliable resources, which help me prepare for my international nursing licence efficiently.

### O｜观点类

- 触发词：why, how, plans, other broad questions
- 骨架（15词）：Personally, I would say [answer]. The main reason is [reason], and [detail] supports that view.
- 完整示例（28词）：I would buy a reliable car rather than an expensive one. Safety and running costs matter most to me, and comparing prices helps me make a practical decision.

## 四、12个个人素材模块

记忆时抓住每段的“人物／场景／结果”，不必逐字复述。每次回答从一个模块抽取一至两句即可。

### M1｜护士工作与繁忙日程

覆盖主题：Tidiness, Memory, Morning Routines, Life stages
背诵段（33词）：I am a nurse, so my schedule is hectic. Staying organised helps me manage shifts and exam preparation. I check my plan and use reminders so that I do not miss something important.

### M2｜阅读、音乐与放松

覆盖主题：Music, Reading, Childhood activities, Morning Routines
背诵段（32词）：Reading and gentle music are ways to unwind. After a shift, they help me switch off and recharge my batteries. I enjoy sad Cantonese songs because their stories feel meaningful and nostalgic.

### M3｜公园、散步与自然

覆盖主题：Public gardens and parks, Walking, The area you live in
背诵段（33词）：There is a park by my apartment where I walk. Its paths and air help me stay active and reduce stress. If I had time, I would explore coastal paths with beautiful scenery.

### M4｜童年收藏与朋友

覆盖主题：Childhood activities, Life stages
背诵段（33词）：As a child, I loved showing my jewellery collection to friends. We exchanged necklaces and earrings. Spending time together gave me a sense of belonging, and those simple moments still make me nostalgic.

### M5｜大学女子赛艇队

覆盖主题：Sports team
背诵段（33词）：At college, I led an all-female rowing team which trained on the sea. Working towards one goal helped us build a strong rapport. I remember those demanding but exciting days with genuine pride.

### M6｜历史老师与小熊礼物

覆盖主题：Teachers, Gifts, History-related questions
背诵段（32词）：My favourite teacher taught history at school. After I failed an exam, she comforted me with a teddy bear. It was her kindness that restored my confidence, and I still keep it.

### M7｜父亲、面包和蛋糕

覆盖主题：Food, Gifts, Childhood activities
背诵段（31词）：Bread and cake have been my favourites since childhood. My father bought them for breakfast after work. Those treats made me feel cared for, so they matter more than their price.

### M8｜Apple Watch、网站、耳机与打字

覆盖主题：Watch, Websites, Headphones, Typing, Memory
背诵段（32词）：My Apple Watch and online tools streamline my routine. I use them for messages, schedules, resources and reminders. They save time, although I avoid headphones or screens when safety and concentration matter.

### M9｜购物、比价与实用选择

覆盖主题：Shopping, Cars
背诵段（32词）：I enjoy shopping, but I compare prices and focus on practical value. The same applies to cars: reliability, safety and reasonable cost matter more to me than a luxury brand or appearance.

### M10｜科学、护理学习与太空兴趣

覆盖主题：Science, Outer space and stars
背诵段（33词）：Biology interests me because it connects with nursing and patient care. Science strengthens my thinking, while documentaries simplify ideas. Space also fascinates me, although I would only travel there if it became safe.

### M11｜笑话、人际关系、礼物与宠物

覆盖主题：Jokes & Comedies, Gifts, Pets and Animals, The area you live in
背诵段（34词）：Laughter and thoughtful gifts help people maintain close bonds. Pets provide companionship, but owning one requires responsibility. Since my schedule is busy, I would only adopt an animal when I could care for it.

### M12｜国际护士执照与五年目标

覆盖主题：Life stages, Websites, Teachers
背诵段（34词）：My goal is to complete my international nursing licence and develop professionally. Over the next five years, I want to protect my health and make time for family. Realistic goals help me maintain momentum.

## 五、16个高频连接表达

| 功能 | 可直接调用的表达 |
|---|---|
| 条件 | If I had the chance, I would… |
| 条件 | If it became safe, I would… |
| 定语从句 | which helps me… |
| 定语从句 | where I can… |
| There-be | There was a time when… |
| There-be | There are several reasons why… |
| 强调 | It was … that … |
| 话题切换 | When it comes to… |
| 话题切换 | In terms of… |
| 原因 | mainly because… |
| 补充 | especially when… |
| 补充 | On top of that, … |
| 对比 | whereas… |
| 对比 | rather than… |
| 对比 | Compared with… |
| 结果 | As a result, … |

使用规则：一次回答只选一个主结构，再加一处简单连接。比如“条件句＋whereas”已经足够，不要把多个复杂句型堆在一起。

## 七、60分钟背诵流程

| 时间 | 任务 | 完成标准 |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–10分钟 | 八类开头 | 看到触发词，两秒内说出对应开头。 |
| 10–40分钟 | 12个素材模块 | 每模块约2.5分钟：读两遍、遮住复述、压缩成两句。 |
| 40–50分钟 | 16个连接表达 | 每种语法功能挑一个最顺口的表达造句。 |
| 50–60分钟 | 随机抽题 | 每类至少抽两题；只看索引，在3秒内开口，25–45词结束。 |

### 最后10分钟自检

- 第一秒是否直接回答，而不是复述题目？
- 是否只给一个清楚原因？
- 是否加入一个符合本人经历的细节？
- 是否在45词左右主动收尾？
- 是否只使用一种自然的复杂结构？

## 八、组合演练抽样

下面每类选两题。操作方法是：先念建议开头，再从指定模块抽一句原因和一句细节。答案可以变化，不必追求唯一版本。

- **L 喜好**：“Do you like watching team games? Why?” → M5；“What are your favourite activities?” → M2
- **F 频率**：“How often do you go shopping?” → M9；“Do you type on a desktop or laptop keyboard every day?” → M8
- **E 经历**：“Have you ever been part of a sports team?” → M5；“What were your favourite activities when you were a child?” → M4
- **I 重要性**：“Do you think that it is necessary to be tidy?” → M1；“Do you think it is important to wear a watch? Why?” → M8
- **N 本国现象**：“Is team sports popular in your culture?” → M5；“Are comedy shows popular in your country?” → M11
- **C 选择对比**：“What are the differences between team sports and individual sports?” → M5；“Did you prefer to do activities alone or with a group of people when you were a child?” → M4
- **G 列举**：“What kinds of websites do you often visit?” → M8；“What type of headphones do you use?” → M8
- **O 观点类**：“How do you keep your work or study space tidy?” → M1；“Do you eat different foods at different times of the year?” → M7

完成标准：16道抽样题都能在3秒内开始，并用“直接回应＋原因＋细节”完成25–45词回答。若卡住，先回到素材模块记关键词，不要重新背原来的长答案。
